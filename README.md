### ClinixSol — AI-Powered Healthcare Platform

A full-stack healthcare platform connecting patients, doctors, laboratories, and pharmacies through integrated digital workflows.

**Key Features**
- AI-generated summaries of medical test reports.
- Online doctor consultations and appointments.
- Digital prescriptions sent directly to pharmacies.
- Online laboratory test booking and report management.
- Patient medical records and healthcare coordination.

**Tech Stack:** React.js, Node.js, REST APIs, Authentication, Database Integration, AI Integration, monogodb
